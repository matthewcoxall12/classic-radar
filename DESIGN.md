# ClassicsGo design direction
Premium motoring editorial meets useful event discovery. Ivory space, racing green, restrained brass and real documentary photography. Cormorant Garamond display headlines paired with locally served DM Sans. No gradients or decorative dashboards.

## References
Goodwood motorsport: cinematic motoring photography and editorial hierarchy. Classic & Sports Car: readable magazine composition. Hagerty UK: stories connected to enthusiast utility. References informed composition; no assets or copy were copied.

## System
The shared masthead, event cards, search controls, editorial sections, essential-details panel and responsive grids form the system. Tokens are in design-tokens.json and Tailwind configuration. Mobile navigation collapses at 1100px; cards become single column at 560px. Refine filters are native keyboard accessible details, categories use real checkboxes, and lists have a lazy loaded OpenStreetMap view. Account and administrative workflows retain their existing authorization and data actions.

## Content integrity
Only published Supabase events are displayed. Verification is explicit. Images are historic or illustrative and credited; assets stay under their original Creative Commons licences. No invented club records. Club directory, route planning, alerts and Roadbook billing are clearly upcoming. Free saves, attendance and submissions remain operational.

## Photography
See /photography for original source links, authors, licences and adapted image downloads. New image assets are compressed WebP. Event photographs are illustrative fallbacks and are not represented as organiser photographs.

## Opening image refinement
The smaller homepage opening image shows an imagined informal village-hall meet with an Austin A35 van, everyday cars and people with tea. It is labelled as AI generated and is not used as event evidence. The hero now has inset margins and a 3:2 image rather than the previous full-height photograph.
